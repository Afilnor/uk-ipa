# UK IPA Pop-up: privacy policy

_Last updated: 29 September 2026_

**UK IPA Pop-up collects no data.** The words you highlight never leave your device: pronunciations come from
a dictionary built into the extension, so lookups work offline and the extension makes no network requests.

- There is no account and no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
- The extension stores one value in your browser's synced extension storage: the date you installed it.
  It is used only to recognise you as an existing user when the extension is updated. It never leaves your
  browser's storage (and your browser account's sync, if you use it). Uninstalling the extension removes it.
- The `storage` permission is used only for that value. Access to all websites is needed only to notice which
  word you highlighted and show the pop-up next to it.

If a future version changes what is described here, this page will be updated before that version is released.
